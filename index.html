Fabric Guide & Client Reviews

Pure Egyptian Lawn

80/80 High-Density Weave

Extremely lightweight, breathable, and silky-smooth against the skin. Ideal for warm weather with superior color-fastness that preserves intricate resham embroidery.

Care: Cold hand wash with mild detergent. Avoid prolonged direct sunlight.

Mercerized Combed Cotton

Structured All-Season Comfort

Combed long-staple cotton treated for lustrous sheen and anti-wrinkle resilience. Crisp drape that holds sharp tailoring and Mandarin collar silhouettes.

Care: Machine wash delicate. Iron on medium cotton setting.

Handwoven Slub Khaddar

Textured Winter Warmth

Richly textured Pakistani khaddar with artisanal horizontal slub yarns. Provides comfortable insulation for autumn and winter festivities.

Care: Pre-shrink in cold water before stitching. Dry clean recommended.

Summer Blended Linen

Natural Breathable Drape

Natural flax fibers blended with cotton for a soft earthy texture that breathes effortlessly. Perfect for fluid relaxed A-line kurtis.

Care: Steam iron while damp for effortless crease recovery.

Everyday Minimalist Chic

Pair with: Meher or Hania Lawn Kurti

Pair with clean white cigarette pants, classic Kolhapuri chappals, and minimal pearl studs for effortless office or daytime grace.

Formal Evening & Dinners

Pair with: Noor Embroidered or Pareesa Zari Kurti

Style with matching straight-cut raw silk trousers, metallic gold khussa, and a lightweight chiffon dupatta.

Festive & Dawat Edit

Pair with: Maham Champagne or Zoya Crimson Kurti

Our Craft Philosophy

Designed for Pakistani Style

Velour Wears focuses exclusively on authentic Pakistani Kurti designs, sourcing pure breathable lawn, linen, and cotton fabrics, paired with intricate resham threadwork tailored to contemporary silhouettes.

Artisanal Needlework

Every Kurti features meticulously executed resham, chikan, or gold tilla needlework inspired by traditional Pakistani craft, finished with clean neckline trims and tailored side vents.

Pure Breathable Textiles

We choose 80/80 luxury lawn, mercerized combed cotton, slub khaddar, and textured summer linen. Tested for drape, color fastness, and effortless everyday comfort across Pakistani climates.

Curated Small & Large Sizing

To eliminate size ambiguity and ensure flattering silhouettes, our collections are precision-tailored into standardized Small and Large profiles with verified chest, shoulder, and length proportions.
